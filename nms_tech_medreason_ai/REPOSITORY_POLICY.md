# Repository Use Policy

This repository is intended for authorized NMS Tech development, review,
evaluation, and approved collaboration.

Unless NMS Tech gives prior written authorization, users may not:
- mirror or republish the repository;
- copy substantial proprietary portions into another project;
- remove or alter copyright notices;
- distribute modified copies;
- sell or sublicense proprietary NMS Tech materials;
- present NMS Tech proprietary materials as independently authored work;
- upload protected third-party medical textbooks or licensed documents without permission.

Nothing in this policy restricts rights granted directly by third-party software
licences for third-party components themselves.
