# Clinical Safety Disclaimer

MedReason AI is an experimental clinical-reasoning software prototype for research, education and clinician-support workflow development.

It is NOT:
- a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional;
- an autonomous diagnostic system;
- a prescription system;
- a validated medical device;
- a replacement for local protocols, specialist consultation, or emergency services.

The system may be incomplete, outdated, or wrong. Never delay emergency care because of an AI output.

Do not enter names, phone numbers, addresses, hospital numbers, photographs, or other directly identifying patient information.

Before real-world clinical deployment, perform appropriate clinical validation, privacy/cybersecurity review, regulatory assessment, risk management and human-factors evaluation.
