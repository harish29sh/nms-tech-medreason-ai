# NMS Tech Notice

This repository combines proprietary NMS Tech project material with third-party software dependencies and external medical-reference links.

External medical guidelines are referenced as **link-only** by default. They are not copied into the NMS Tech RAG corpus unless a licence review explicitly marks the source as permitted for the intended use.

Do not upload Harrison's, Bailey & Love, or other copyrighted textbooks into the RAG corpus without appropriate permission/licensing.
