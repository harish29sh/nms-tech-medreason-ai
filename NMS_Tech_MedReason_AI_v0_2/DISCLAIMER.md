# Clinical Safety Disclaimer

NMS Tech MedReason AI v0.2 is a research/education and clinician-support software prototype. It is **not a validated medical device**, autonomous diagnostic system, prescription service, or substitute for clinical judgment.

The system may be incomplete, incorrect, or outdated. Emergency care and local clinical protocols must never be delayed because of an AI response.

Use synthetic or de-identified cases during development. Do not enter direct patient identifiers.

Before real-world clinical deployment, obtain appropriate clinical validation, privacy/cybersecurity review, human-factors testing, quality/risk management, and regulatory/legal assessment.
